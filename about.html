About

Why Renovare exists

Many people who need a computer can't afford one, and good machines are thrown away every day. Renovare connects the two.

What we do

We accept donated laptops and computers, including ones that are old or no longer work. Each one is assessed, and anything that can be repaired or upgraded is brought back to working order. Upgrades are usually extra memory or a faster drive.

We then install a free Linux operating system, which runs well on older hardware and is simple to use. Every device goes out with a charger, a short plain-English guide, and a contact for help.

Our standards

Every drive is securely erased and the erasure is recorded.
Chargers are tested, and damaged or unbranded ones are not passed on.
Batteries that are swollen or unsafe are recycled responsibly.
Devices are given away, not sold.

Who it's for

We pass devices to local organisations that already work with people who need them, such as community groups, support services and family projects. Those groups decide who receives a device, because they know their communities best.

The young person behind it

Renovare is run by a [14]-year-old volunteer in [Cambridge], with a parent as the responsible adult. He's doing it to support his community, and he's learning a lot along the way about hardware, security and working with people.

Adult volunteers help with collections, deliveries and any dealings with organisations. If you'd like to get involved, please get in touch.
