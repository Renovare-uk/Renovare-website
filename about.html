About

Why it exists

Good computers get thrown away every week while families, young carers and new arrivals struggle without one. Renovare sits between the two.

What happens to a laptop

Each machine is checked on arrival. Anything that can be repaired or upgraded is brought back to working order, usually with more memory or a faster drive.

We install a free Linux system, which runs well on older hardware, and pack it with a charger and a plain-English guide.

Our standards

Every drive is securely erased and the erasure is recorded.

Chargers are tested. Damaged or unbranded ones are not passed on.

Swollen batteries are removed and recycled, never reused.

Devices are given away, never sold.

Who gets them

We give devices to local groups that already work with people who need them: community groups, support services and family projects. Those groups decide who receives a device, because they know their communities best.

Who runs it

Renovare is a volunteer project run by a young person in Cambridge, with an adult taking responsibility for it. Adults handle collections, deliveries and every conversation with an organisation.
