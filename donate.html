Donate

Give a device a second life

We take laptops, desktop computers, monitors, and spare memory or drives. Working and broken devices are both welcome.

Drop-off

Drop-off point: to be confirmed

Until it's confirmed, please email us and we'll arrange a time that suits you.

Collection

For larger amounts, we can collect within Cambridge and surrounding area.

We accept

Laptops and desktop computers, working or not

Monitors in working condition

Spare memory and drives

Genuine chargers in good condition

We can't accept

Devices with a management lock or BIOS password, unless the owner supplies the credentials

Swollen or damaged batteries

Monitors with cracked panels

Unbranded or damaged chargers

Before you donate

Remove personal accounts if you can. We erase every drive regardless, and we'll confirm the erasure in writing if you ask.

We don't take cash donations at the moment. If you'd like to help with spare parts, email us first.
