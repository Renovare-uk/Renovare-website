Donate

Give a device a second life

We accept laptops, desktop computers, monitors, and spare memory or drives. Working and non-working devices are both welcome.

How to donate

Drop-off

Bring your device to [your local drop off point - TBC].

Collection

For larger amounts, get in touch and we'll arrange a time to collect, within [area].

Please remove any personal accounts first if you can. We'll securely erase everything regardless, and we'll confirm the erasure if you ask.

Please don't send money.
Renovare doesn't currently take cash donations. If you'd like to help with spare parts, memory or drives, please get in touch first.

What we can't accept

Devices with a management lock or BIOS password, unless the owner provides the credentials. Devices with damaged or swollen batteries. Monitors with cracked panels. Unbranded or damaged chargers.
