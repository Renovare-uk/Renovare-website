The process

Four stages. Nothing hidden.

01

Collect

Working and broken laptops and computers from households, schools and local businesses.

02

Wipe

Every drive is securely erased and the erasure is logged. Nobody's data is kept or opened.

03

Restore

Cleaned, tested, upgraded where it helps, and fitted with a free Linux system and a charger.

04

Pass on

Devices go to local groups who already know who needs them, with a simple guide to get started.
