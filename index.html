Renovare collects donated laptops and computers around Cambridge, wipes and repairs them, and passes them to local groups who work with people who need a device. Each one leaves with a tested charger and a plain guide.

Four stages, in order

01

Collect

Drop-off or collection within Cambridge and the surrounding area. We take laptops and desktops, working or not, plus spare memory, drives, working monitors and genuine chargers.

Drop-off and collection arrangements are made by email.

Animation, stage 01: to come

02

Wipe

Every drive is fully erased, whatever state the device is in. The erasure is recorded for each device.

Remove personal accounts first if you can. We erase the drive either way.

Animation, stage 02: to come

03

Restore

Faults are repaired where that is practical, and a free Linux system is installed. Chargers are tested.

Some devices get more memory or a faster SSD where that makes a real difference. What gets fixed depends on the device, and we do not promise a repair.

Animation, stage 03: to come

04

Pass on

Devices go to local groups who already work with people who need them: community groups, support services and family projects. They are given, not sold.

Each one leaves with a tested charger and a plain-English guide.

Animation, stage 04: to come
