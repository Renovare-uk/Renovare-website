Refurbished technology, volunteer-run

Giving computers a second life.

We take old or broken laptops and computers, bring them back to working order, and pass them to people who have less access to technology than most of us do.

Donate a device How it works

01

We collect

Working or broken laptops and computers, from households, schools and local businesses.

02

We restore

Every drive is securely wiped, tested, cleaned, and fitted with a free Linux system and a charger.

03

We pass them on

Devices go to local groups who know who needs them, with a simple guide to get started.

Started by a teenager who wanted to help.

Renovare is a volunteer project run by a young person in our community, with a parent taking responsibility for it. It began when he noticed how many good computers were being thrown away while local families struggled to get online for school, work and appointments.

Read more about the project →

Every device is wiped before it leaves us.
We never open or keep anyone's data. Each machine is logged, tested, and handed over with a guide and a contact for help.
