The process

Four stages. Nothing hidden.

01

Collect

We take working and broken laptops and computers from households, schools and local businesses. Collection can be arranged for larger amounts.

Each device is logged on arrival, with its make, model and condition noted, so we know what we have before we start.

02

Wipe

Every drive is securely erased before anything else happens. We never open, copy or keep anyone's files.

The erasure is recorded in our log against the device, so there's a clear trail for every machine.

03

Restore

Devices are cleaned and tested. Where it makes a real difference, we upgrade them with more memory or a faster drive.

Each one gets a free Linux system, which runs well on older hardware, and is checked to boot properly.

04

Pass on

Devices go to local groups who already work with people who need them, such as community groups, support services and family projects.

Each one leaves with a charger, a plain-English guide and a contact for help if something goes wrong.
