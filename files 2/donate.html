Donate a device.

Remove personal accounts if you can. We erase every drive regardless.

Drop-off
Drop-off point to be confirmed. Email us to arrange it.
Collection
Available within Cambridge and the surrounding area. Email us to arrange a time.

We accept

  • Laptops and desktops, working or not
  • Working monitors
  • Spare memory and drives
  • Genuine chargers in good condition

We cannot accept

  • Management locks or BIOS passwords, unless the owner supplies the credentials
  • Swollen or damaged batteries
  • Cracked monitors
  • Unbranded or damaged chargers
