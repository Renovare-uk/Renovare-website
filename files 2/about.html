Working laptops end up in drawers.

People who need one often can't buy one. Renovare sits between the two.

Photo: a stack of old laptops, before
Photo: the same stack, wiped and sorted

What happens to a laptop

It is collected and its drive is fully erased. It is repaired where that is practical and given a free Linux system. It then goes to a local group, with a tested charger and a plain-English guide.

Our standards

Erasure
Recorded for every device.
Chargers
Tested before they leave.
Batteries
Recycled.
Devices
Given, not sold.

Who gets them

Community groups
Local groups who already work with people who need a device.
Support services
Services that already support people in the area.
Family projects
Projects that need a working device.

Who runs it

Run by a young volunteer, with an adult taking responsibility for the project.

Renovare is a volunteer community project and not a registered charity. It does not take cash and does not claim tax relief or charitable status.
